Aloe vera

Aloe Vera

Desert leaves hiding cooling gel.

01About this plant

Aloe vera stores water in its thick, serrated leaves — a desert survival trick that also gives us the cooling gel inside. Snap a leaf and the clear gel is ready for skin and hair.

02Uses

  • Gel for burns and skincare
  • Juices and health drinks
  • Ornamental succulent pots

03Where it grows

  • Hot, dry climates
  • Sandy, well-drained soil
  • Very low water needs

04Plant care

  • Plant in sandy, fast-draining mix
  • Water deeply, then let dry fully
  • Repot pups into their own pots

05Interesting facts

  • Aloe gel is 99% water, plus vitamins and enzymes.
  • It closes its leaf pores at night to save water — a CAM plant.
